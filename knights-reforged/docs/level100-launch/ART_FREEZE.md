# Owner artwork freeze — 2026-10-25

Stop generation, recoloring, reframing and recycling of expansion designs.
The owner will provide separately approved, original equipment artwork later.
Engineering, population validation and release compatibility continue independently.

All 42 ordinary expansion paintings (36 shop items and six Land drafts) are
frozen and unapproved; the prior pending selection is rejected. Preserve every
existing file as reference evidence. The earlier galleries retain their historical
labels: **“pending” in those images does not mean acceptable for adoption now**.
The latest four generated trials are archived byte-for-byte as unapproved PNGs in
`art/frozen-trials-20261025/`. They are not runtime replacements. No further art
changes or alternative concepts are authorized by this engineering checkpoint.

The three exact, owner-selected randomized-name Hudson PNGs remain preserved
without repainting or substitution. Their final framing/rights approval is still
separate from approval of the rest of the expansion. Existing approved classic
artwork, Crown Harbour and Phase 5.1B assets remain unchanged.

Every item at every level must have a dark textured background consistent with
the approved game art. Levels 60–100 are not an exception. Future prestige must
come from distinct silhouette, construction, material and craftsmanship, not
brighter backgrounds or additional glow. This rule does not authorize altering
any currently approved artwork during the freeze.

[Frozen inventory and hashes](art-freeze.json) bind the current bytes and the
unadopted trials. The installed, historical prepared manifest is preserved;
it has no final approvals and cannot pass `expansionAssetReadiness()`. Do not
mark its entries approved or use old gallery labels as authorization. Final
owner-supplied art must use a new reviewed immutable art version with correct
formats, per-ID mappings, provenance and full-bleed frame checks before launch.

**Artwork is a launch dependency, not a reason to stop E3 engineering.** This
freeze does not activate content, change stats, authorize population capital,
create production bots, grant Hudson equipment, merge or deploy anything.
