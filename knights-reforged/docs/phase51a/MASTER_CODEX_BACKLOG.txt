KINGDOM COME — MASTER CODEX BACKLOG AND VISION
Compiled October 21, 2026. Planning reference, NOT authorization to merge, deploy or change production.

NORTH STAR
Faithful evolution of Storm8 Kingdoms Live / Knights Reforged: simple, persistent, competitive. Five fixed tabs: Home, Quests, Battle, Armory, Recruit. Army size matters strongly alongside gear and skills; an 80-member army should not routinely beat a comparable 150-member army. Keep original Kingdom Come artwork and charcoal/bronze/gold styling. Compact, legible, information-dense mobile UX. Make actions consequential, rivalries memorable and information immediately accessible. Avoid elaborate skill trees, battle passes, crafting, chore overload and feature creep. Human rivalry should eventually dominate; bots keep the early realm active without pretending to be humans.

1. UPKEEP / HOURLY INCOME — IMPORTANT NEW ECONOMY CHANGE
When a player's exposed wallet is below equipment upkeep due, use actually earned hourly Land income at the ordinary 60-minute settlement to cover upkeep before paying out the remainder. If legitimate available funds suffice, equipment should not remain suspended merely because the wallet was low before the income tick. No advances, Vault withdrawal, free upkeep, negative balances or duplicate charges. Inspect existing maintenance reserve, suspension/resume, income cursor, fractional credits, offline catch-up and ledger semantics first. Determine exact auto-resume timing; user expects no need to manually press Resume Upkeep every hour. Test insufficient income, combined wallet+income, purchases, raids, concurrency, restart, rollback and exact-once receipts. Requires separately reviewed financial behavior change, not casual UI patch.

2. VICTORY / BATTLE RESULT POLISH
Phase 4.9.1 result redesign is approved. Preserve real equipment snapshots, both armies, expanded details, Attack Again/Revenge eligibility and current formulas. Tighten Gold Won and XP Gained panels; +182/+318 alone looks barren. Use existing coin and XP icons, concise labels and restrained motion respecting reduced-motion. Distinguish 0 gold and reduced XP honestly. Shorten repeated Attack Again explanatory text. Add compact level-up Congratulations banner integrated into result, not disruptive modal. Visually separate actual bounty claim and amount from ordinary loot. Fix ambiguous battle/bounty wording; preserve historical reports with missing snapshots gracefully. No changes to rewards without separate decision.

3. BULK BUY/SELL AND ARMORY DENSITY
Bulk dialog is too tall. Aim for ~20–25% less vertical space, keeping ~44px usable mobile touch targets, readable totals and confirmation. Show REAL existing item art beside title for weapons/armor/spells and relevant other items, including Stormglass Pike. Compact +/- controls, 1/5/10/Max presets, buy/sell columns, labels and spacing. Handle narrow phones and large quantities. Keep price, ownership, upkeep and transactions unchanged. Display more equipment per screen without tiny illegible text.

4. LEGACY VISUAL CLEANUP
Eliminate generic bright green bubble/pill/button styling in favor of consistent charcoal/bronze/gold and restrained neutral UI. Keep teal Health/Mana/Spirit resource bars and semantic status colors. Preserve original art, existing five-tab navigation and medieval identity; don't copy old Storm8 artwork or layouts pixel-for-pixel. Reduce excessive padding, huge cards, wasted vertical space and unnecessary scrolling.

5. PLAYER PROFILES / IDENTITY
Compact profile should immediately show level, Calling, victories/losses, tracked kills/deaths, quests, account age ('playing for X days'), equipment and earned accomplishments. Do not invent missing stats. Make gross income, upkeep and net/hour clear; expose meaningful paused-upkeep status. Use expandable secondary details, not an oversized new screen. Preserve Player/Skills/Comments/Battles structure. Add distinctive small ORIGINAL framed emblems for Tank, Barbarian, Mage; user rejected earlier generic fantasy emblem proposals. Review art direction before adoption. Reputation should emerge from actual stats/achievements, not new currency or rankings. Existing achievements can receive meaningful one-time milestones.

6. SOCIAL FLOW / RIVALRIES / BOUNTIES
Optimize attack -> result -> opponent profile -> comments/bounty/revenge -> battle, with few taps. Home Recent Activity must contain ONLY events relevant to the logged-in player; no global bot-to-bot events. Compact personal All/Battles/Comments entries with truthful outcomes, timestamps, opponent, Attack/Revenge/Profile actions. Head-to-head rivalry summary belongs in existing profile Battles tab, not new large panel. Bounty List already IS the bounty board; don't duplicate. Show bounty placement, claim, escrow, payout and claimant distinctly. Review repeated placement/claim farming and target-pressure limits. Preserve legitimate transactions and history. Profile comments: clear author/time, readable Unicode and emoji, compact wall, report/block actions and rate limiting. Blocking should not silently grant PvP immunity. Original screenshots show spam and contradictory 'slain but WON' language; do not replicate.

7. SKILLS / PROGRESSION
Keep straightforward point allocation with clear descriptions for resource capacities, Attack and Defense. Preserve viable offensive, defensive and balanced strategies; do not introduce skill trees. Test extremes at levels 100 and 200 before changing formulas. Army size remains a major factor but gear and skills also matter. Future content to level 100 then 200 requires proper quests, Land, gear, XP and economic testing; not authorized by reference screenshots alone. Avoid arbitrary army caps in future design, but removal/scaling is not currently authorized. Wealthy kingdoms should not automatically become defenseless farm targets.

8. MOBILE UX / ONBOARDING / AUDIO
Test small and large Android portrait sizes, safe areas, font scaling, fixed bottom tabs, scroll, keyboard, WebView and real-device audio. Navigation honk was confirmed gone after 4.9.1; do not reintroduce. New players default muted, existing preferences persist, orchestral cues remain. Tutorial/onboarding overhaul explicitly SHELVED. When reconsidered: comprehensive but very fast and non-overbearing, never a 10-minute chore.
