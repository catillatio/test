# Account and Data Deletion

## Request deletion

Use the public /account-deletion page, also linked from Settings. Confirm your existing account name/password and explicitly request deletion. This verifies control without collecting a new email address. A repeated pending request returns the original request receipt.

## What the request does

It queues an authenticated request for account and associated-data deletion. It is not deactivation, does not claim deletion has completed and does not execute destructive operations automatically. You may still sign in while the request is pending.

## Fulfillment requirements

Credentials, sessions, identifiable character/profile data, social content, reports and links must be reviewed for erasure or irreversible de-identification. Shared battle reports, financial ledgers, bounty escrow and other players’ records cannot be blindly cascade-deleted. Any retained record needs a documented legitimate purpose, minimized fields, access controls and a defined expiry.

## Proposed abandoned kingdoms

Not yet active: fulfillment would delete the human login account and personal/social data without immediately creating a successor. A separate approved operation may later create a delayed batch of standardized public abandoned kingdoms, with no copied personal configuration, historical activity or assignment to a former player. These accountless NPCs cannot log in, be reclaimed, post, recruit, buy, spend or attack autonomously. New wallets start at zero and earn future Land income; former wallet and Vault funds remain sealed in restricted integrity records. This is not a promise of legal anonymity: retained records, aggregate timing, backups and external knowledge require independent privacy/legal review and narrowly justified retention before activation.

## Backups and recovery

Encrypted backups and recovery copies require a documented expiry and erasure-reapplication process before any restored copy becomes active. This feature does not change existing recovery protections or authorize recovery-copy retirement.

## Owner completion required

Publisher, support/alternative identity-verification route, response time, retention periods, legal exceptions and the operational fulfillment procedure must be approved and implemented before claiming deletion-policy compliance. See the release-readiness report. Do not send passwords to support or in public messages.
