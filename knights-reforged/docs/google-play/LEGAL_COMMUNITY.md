# Community Guidelines

## Public posting agreement

Review draft, version community-2026-11-05-draft-1. Posting requires explicit acceptance of this version and the Terms. Final publisher/contact details and enforcement policy require owner review before release.

## Prohibited content

Do not post child sexual abuse/exploitation, sexual solicitation, threats, targeted abuse, hate, discriminatory harassment, non-consensual personal information, scams, credential requests, malware, illegal transactions, spam or content infringing another person’s rights. No impersonation of moderators or the publisher.

## Respect and safety

Compete within game rules. Do not turn game rivalry into personal harassment. Public comments and broadcasts are not private messages. Never share passwords, contact details or financial information.

## Reports and blocks

Use Report on a message or Report player on a human profile. Choose the appropriate reason. Block player hides their public social messages from your feeds and prevents comments between the two profiles; manage reversals in Settings. Blocking does not remove historical records, prevent ordinary combat or hide required battle/bounty results.

## Moderation

Author removal, profile-owner hiding, operator hiding/restoration and timed posting restrictions are available and auditable. Reports must be reviewed by the designated moderation operator; reviewer staffing, response expectations, escalation and appeal/contact path: [OWNER TO COMPLETE]. Report volume alone does not establish wrongdoing.
