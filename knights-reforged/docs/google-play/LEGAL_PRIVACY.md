## Account and gameplay data

The application stores your account name, an Argon2id password hash, character/display name, account and character identifiers, progression, resources, equipment, property, army links and game preferences. Passwords are verified on the server; the application does not need camera, contacts, location, microphone or advertising-ID permissions.

## Social content and safety

Comments and broadcasts are public to other signed-in players. The service stores message content, authorship, timestamps, reports, moderation actions, posting-policy acceptance and your social block list. Do not post personal or confidential information. Blocking controls social visibility and contact; it does not change battle eligibility or game rewards.

## Sessions and device storage

The first-party kr_session cookie is HTTP-only, SameSite=Lax and Secure on HTTPS. Server sessions retain a token hash, CSRF token, creation/expiry and activity times. Authenticated cookies have a 30-day lifetime; the server also enforces a seven-day inactivity condition. The WebView uses first-party cookies and DOM storage for the website; third-party cookies are disabled. Signing out revokes the current server session.

## Operations and providers

Traffic is transmitted over HTTPS to the Kingdom Come service hosted on Railway. PostgreSQL stores account/game records. Encrypted recovery artifacts and independently restored recovery copies exist. Runtime HTTP logging may process request/network metadata; provider-side logging, access, locations, retention and subprocessors require owner confirmation. No analytics, advertising, billing or push SDK is bundled in the reviewed native client. This is not a claim that providers collect no operational data.

## Purpose and disclosure

Account data enables sign-in, server-authoritative gameplay, security, support and moderation. Public names, profiles and social content are shown to other players. Provider access, legal grounds, international transfers, legal disclosures and any additional integrations: [OWNER TO REVIEW]. No sale/sharing declaration is made on your behalf.

## Deletion and retention

Request account and associated-data deletion through Settings or /account-deletion. A request is not completed deletion. Protected fulfillment deletes the login account/password hash, revokes sessions, removes reviewed personal/social data and mutable progress, and clears old profile traits and timestamps. Disposable profile/loadout/progression records and unreferenced private asset rows are removed; any historically referenced asset rows have no live quantities. Minimal private references, sealed virtual balances and original financial/combat/asset facts may need limited retention to protect other players, investigate security/fraud or reconcile recovery; they are not automatically anonymous. Necessary categories, purposes, access and retention periods, backup expiry and response time must be accurately approved and disclosed: [OWNER TO COMPLETE]. Account deletion never creates or funds an NPC.
