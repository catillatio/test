# Kingdom Come artwork requirements

Owner requirement, 2026-10-23. Applies permanently to all game artwork, every
shape and resolution, including new assets and future revisions.

- Artwork fills its allocated viewport edge to edge. No black borders,
  letterboxing, pillarboxing, empty gutters, artificial mattes, blurred sidebars
  or mirrored-edge fills. Naturally dark painted scenery is acceptable.
- Preserve defining architecture, silhouettes and landmarks. Do not fix a ratio
  mismatch by cutting off the subject, stretching or disproportionate scaling.
- When ordinary proportional cropping is insufficient, create a properly
  composed scene or extend the real painted environment. Do not add empty fills.
- Retain the approved painterly fantasy identity. Scene extensions/recompositions
  need source/output provenance and owner visual review before adoption.
- Validate native Land frames (68×49, 80×58, 90×60, 198×116), Profile (60×60) and
  receipts (64×72), plus rendered 320/390/430/1024px layouts. Check actual image
  content as well as CSS coverage: `cover` alone cannot certify safe composition.
- Preserve unselected artwork and gameplay. Any existing conflict must be
  reported separately; this policy does not authorize unrelated art replacements.

The Phase 5.1B `985171b` portrait-contain/matte checkpoint was rejected and must
not be used as precedent. Its historical evidence remains available in git.
