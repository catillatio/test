## Smallest implementation slice

1. Extract the existing normal-attack transaction body in `src/commands.ts` into one internal resolution function. Keep the current authenticated human wrapper and its receipt/identity checks unchanged. Add a separate server-only bot-duel wrapper that accepts **only two distinct active registered bots with login-disabled accounts**. Never enable bot logins, impersonate a human or accept a bot actor through a browser intent. The new populated test proves that today's player command path rejects bot actors and rolls back the attempted receipt, both with development rivals enabled and disabled.
2. Use current state and sorted character locks, normal Land/upkeep/resource materialization, level matching, actual participating equipment, Health/Spirit costs and recovery, combat rolls, damage, and persistent attack timing. Reuse `resolveAlphaCombat`, `recordVictory`, `botVictoryRewards`, `ledger`/`creditGold`, `grantExperience` and normal `battles` persistence. Retain both equipment snapshots, actual wallet debits/credits, outcome, damage, XP and level changes. Do not fabricate feed events, combat records, equipment or money.
3. Preserve `bot-combat-rewards-2`: 3% exposed-gold base, existing bot ceiling, protected Vaults, 65% difficulty XP and the fixed-pair 100/85/70/55/40 reductions. Only actual attacking victories advance the ordered pair. Keep the initial defender-bot XP behavior (zero) and human rewards/bounties unchanged. Attacker leveling uses ordinary progression and its documented level-up refills, with no free training, army growth or catalog refresh. Saved levels then inform ordinary future purchase eligibility.
4. Give autonomous duels their own immutable scheduling policy, namespace and durable command receipts. Bound attempts globally and per actor, skip missed scheduling slots, and recheck both identities/eligibility under locks. A practical **review proposal**, not an approved numeric policy: at most five candidate duels per five-minute slot and at most two initiations per bot per UTC day, with a separate target-pressure bound. Initially exclude pairs involving active bounty escrow; do not redirect a human-funded bounty to an autonomous claimant. Check expiry/escrow under the existing lock order. These new scheduling/target/escrow decisions require review before implementation/activation.
5. Keep it independently default OFF behind existing private-alpha/development-bot controls. Stop/drain on shutdown, skip busy actors without replacing them with extra candidates, and log only fixed failure categories. No initialization, seed, grant, schema reset, public administrative route or release-workflow change. Human actors and human targets are categorically outside this wrapper.

## Required acceptance tests for that slice

- Real win/loss persisted from ordinary combat; Spirit/damage/recovery and eligible level-up refills verified. Bots can lose; no forced victory or artificial strength multiplier.
- Exact exposed-wallet transfer and net currency conservation, accounting separately for due Land/upkeep; Vaults unchanged, depleted purses yield no invented loot. Existing 65% XP/repeat policy and levels/skill points persist across process restart. Losses/retries do not consume victory sequence eligibility.
- Duplicate commands, reversed pairs, simultaneous purchases/income/human attacks, crashed transactions, replayed windows and multiple workers cannot duplicate a battle, loot or XP. Busy/missed slots stay bounded.
- Human combat outputs and bounty accounting remain identical. Failed/inactive/login-enabled/human/mismatched targets fail closed. Every human account/session/progress row and all 450 bot identities, old inventories/lots and historical records survive.
- Home Recent Activity remains personal: bot-to-bot battles appear in neither an uninvolved human's All nor Battles feed. Actual combatant history may use existing visibility rules; no global bot activity stream.
- Run a bounded representative progression/load experiment before choosing an activation policy. Include cash depletion, injuries, upkeep pauses, level-up refills and newly eligible gear; do not restart giant 30-day studies or change the approved purchase budget merely to make a graph improve.
