# E3 dependency and population boundary

No bot account, identity, capital, inventory, autonomous flag, scheduling window, spending limit or battle policy is changed or provisioned by E2b. The active population/release contract stays exactly **450**. All legacy bots keep legacy progression and their existing 10-participant constraint.

The offline model preserves the first 450 authored synthetic blueprint identities/portfolios and appends 450 **in-memory hypothetical** opponents, giving a 900-opponent research cohort. This is not a seed manifest, live population snapshot or proposed production write. It demonstrates catalog/quantity/eligibility compatibility and explains why the faster progression numbers depend on upper-level opponents.

E3 needs separate owner decisions and implementation for:

1. An immutable new-identity manifest and idempotent provisioning transaction that cannot regenerate or rewrite the existing 450 bots. Independently verify all current IDs/business rows before and after the future expansion.
2. Allocation through levels 51–100, actual consent/recruitment availability, level/Health eligibility and varied difficulty. Define a separate policy for future bots instead of silently lifting every existing bot's army constraint.
3. Explicit capitalization approval. E1's approximately **10.18B gold-equivalent** proposal included wallets/Vault/reserves, equipment and Land; it is not a funding authorization. The actual Approach-A cohort's modeled capitalization is in [population.json](population.json). Reconcile differences and funding provenance before any seeding. No unlimited refill mechanism is proposed.
4. Scheduler/population constants and capacity reviewed for 900, settlement batching, missed scheduling slots, parallel-worker idempotency, bot-versus-bot work, lot/journal growth and restore time. Do not simply double a flag or suppress a count assertion. The unchanged 450-capacity income sweep needs two calls for 900 due bots.
5. New strict expected-population engine and release/restore/preservation contracts, still exact. E2b's activator deliberately rejects a bot count other than 450; a 900-bot rollout cannot bypass it. Any E3 replacement contract must protect the original 450 and explicitly validate the new manifest.
6. Measured volume/recovery headroom, including retained verification copies, WAL, snapshots, hourly journals, acquisition/drop-outcome growth and independent restore capacity. Do not delete recovery copies or change retention infrastructure in E2b. Storage concerns remain a separate owner-reviewed task.
7. Fresh protected exact-commit CI, encrypted recovery, independent restore/preservation rehearsal and explicit owner authorization before provisioning or activation.

The activator also requires at least five available opponents in each conservative ±5-level band around 51/60/70/80/90/100. Active bots or eligible human accounts with at least 20 Health may satisfy it. This is a fail-closed point-in-time floor, not a guarantee of perpetual player availability. Current real population/health has not been inspected. The regular 450-cohort simulation takes 105.54 days; the intended 60–90-day experience therefore must not be advertised as achieved with the current population alone.

No defender rewards, bounty changes, clans, new currencies or bot-initiated human attacks are introduced. Existing combat reward formulas, bot gold and 65% XP policy, pair-repeat reductions, bounties, Vault and one-clock Land accounting remain unchanged.
