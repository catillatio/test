| 500 | 2,000 | 200.0 | 36.24% | 7.54% |
| 1000 | 1,000 | 100.0 | 59.36% | 22.75% |

500 ppm is provisional, with a median first-copy wait of 1,386 eligible quests. At ten eligible quests/day, the mean first copy is 200 days and 15 copies of a specific item average 3,000 days. Five hundred copies would take an impractical average 100,000 days at that rate. The 100/500-copy cases are stress bounds, not promised progression goals. This makes shops remain central but may be too rare for ordinary players; the owner must explicitly choose the activation rate. No rate is approved by installation.
