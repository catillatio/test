# Game Design — Classic Mafia RPG

## Player promise
Start as a small-time criminal, complete quick jobs, accumulate cash, purchase equipment and income-generating businesses, expand a mob, fight rivals, claim hit-list targets, and climb the rankings. Sessions should be fast and decisions meaningful.

## Core loop
1. Collect property/business income and review status.
2. Spend energy on jobs and earn experience/cash.
3. Recruit mob members; buy and allocate offensive, defensive, and vehicle-style equipment.
4. Engage in lightweight menu-driven PvP, rivals, revenge and hit lists.
5. Reinvest into equipment/upkeep and business properties.
6. Progress to higher-level jobs and equipment while preserving early-game accessibility.

## UI character
- Closely evoke classic compact Storm8-style **information density and navigation behavior**, not a wholesale modern card-based redesign.
- Clear stat header: level, health, energy, stamina, cash, and relevant progression meters (final stats require verification).
- Responsive mobile-first layouts with accessible touch hit areas, readable text and immediate interaction feedback.
- Modern, **original** dark urban crime art, distinct silhouettes, and consistent equipment imagery.
- Avoid excessive animations, multi-step dialogs, forced tutorials and sprawling navigation.

## Mappings — starting hypotheses, not final mechanics
| Kingdom Come | Candidate crime RPG analogue |
|---|---|
| Army | Mob |
| Quests | Jobs/crimes |
| Gold | Cash |
| Land | Real estate/businesses |
| Weapons | Crime-era weapons |
| Armor | Defensive gear |
| Spells | Vehicles / other gear category — **verify exact stat/combat fit** |
| Rival battles | Fight list, revenge |
| Bounties | Hit lists |
| Leaderboards | Top mobsters |

Verify the original game's limits, mob size participation, equipment allocation, level gates, health/energy/stamina regeneration, cash loss, fight resolution and account interactions before finalizing the schema.

## Out of scope initially
Large open-world exploration, 3D worlds, city-building RTS systems, unrelated progression currencies, elaborate new combat modes, or extra systems that compromise the original quick-session experience.

## Identity and IP
Use a new title, lore, iconography, assets, copy and design system. Historical games may be studied for gameplay inspiration, but do not lift their protected expression. Review branding/trademark availability before launch.
