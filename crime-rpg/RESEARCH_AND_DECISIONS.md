# Research, Positioning & Decision Log

## Working market hypothesis
An accessible crime/mob narrative may be simpler to communicate in short ads than medieval fantasy; existing mafia-themed mobile games indicate awareness of the theme but **do not establish demand** for a specific classic menu-driven RPG. Treat this as an untested commercial hypothesis.

## Planned positioning
An original, modernized *classic mobile mafia RPG experience*: brief sessions, jobs, mob growth, properties, player fights, rivals and hit lists. Do not market it as an official sequel or claim association with Storm8/iMobsters.

## Discovery methods
- Interview veterans of classic Storm8-style games, without spamming communities.
- Compare publicly archived mechanics/screenshots with modern player expectations.
- Prototype advertising claims and measure qualified signups, onboarding completion and retention.
- Test whether nostalgia positioning reaches more than one narrow audience.
- Compare the crime/fantasy games using observed metrics, not download totals from unrelated genres.

## Decisions (2026-10-29)
- Create this planning folder inside existing Kingdom Come GitHub repository **now**.
- Keep **Kingdom Come launch and players untouched**.
- Once stable, **copy/fork first**, test baseline, **rebalance formulas second**, retheme with independently created crime assets after/alongside mechanics adaptation, and validate through tests.
- Long term: two games running independently, with separate GitHub repository, Railway project, database, domain and accounts.
- Product direction: **almost exact classic iMobsters-style gameplay**, visually modern, rather than sprawling modern mafia empire strategy.

## Open questions (non-blocking for planning)
- Historical formula evidence quality and differences from Kingdom Come
- New product name / branding clearance
- Exact vehicle/equipment slot mapping and PvP participation rules
- Target economy durations and monetization strategy
- Launch platform order, tester recruitment and retention goals

## Change policy
Any future implementation needs explicit owner approval. Keep research distinct from current Kingdom Come operational rules and historical references; do not edit or deploy the existing title during planning.
